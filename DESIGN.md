# Two Minds Academy

## Direction
An independent technical school with the confidence of a design studio. Editorial, airy, asymmetric. The original Two Minds wordmark connects to the agency; the academy introduces a paper-and-lime identity.

## Tokens
Paper #f3f3eb for the canvas. Ink #242622 for text and dark surfaces. Signal #d4ed87 is the single accent. Stone #66685f for secondary text. Rule #d7d9cf for dividers.
Display: local Neue Machina. Body: Arial. Metadata: monospace. Fluid headlines, tight tracking, body measure under 65ch.

## Composition
Max-width 1440px. Split hero with a custom mathematical wire sculpture. Two asymmetric discipline panels. Ruled learning-path rows instead of a generic three-card grid. Single column below 768px. Minimum touch targets 44px. Persistent mobile course/contact actions. Stage-choice controls use a compact two-column button group on phones.

## Interaction
Choose a subject first, then reveal four plainly named courses with explicit detail links. Native dialog for curriculum and enquiries, with Escape dismissal, focus management, direct phone and WhatsApp links. GSAP hero entrances, scroll-linked artwork, and subtle section reveals; scoped cleanup and transform/opacity animation. Respect reduced-motion.

## Content
Lead with student outcomes. No invented testimonials, placement rates, prices, dates, or promises. University help is guided learning, not assignment completion. Cybersecurity exercises use authorized labs.

## Avoid
No neon, purple gradients, emoji icons, generic feature grids, fake social proof, or filler scroll instructions.
